Fixed age-restricted videos being retried five times and then reported as download failures. yt-dlp rewrote the tail of its age-gate error — it used to end `This video may be inappropriate for some users` and now ends `Use --cookies-from-browser or --cookies for the authentication` — and the classifier matched on the whole sentence, so the branch silently stopped firing. `AGE_RESTRICTED` was already a rejection type and already stamped its span `OK`; that code was simply unreachable, and every age-gated video fell through to the retryable fallback instead. The consequences all followed from that one miss: five attempts over roughly twenty minutes on an error no exit can satisfy, a `RETRY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` span stamped `ERROR` that drove the Download Client Errors alert to 100% on a single video during an idle hour, a `FailureStatus` pushed into the queue that feeds the backlog alert and the exponential backoff multiplier, and — worst of the set — a healthy Mullvad exit named in the `Download failure ... attributed to egress exit` line that the runbook tells you to grep when deciding which exits to drop from `egress_exits`. The matcher now pins only the stable leading sentence, and the test covers both wordings so the next rewording of the tail cannot un-match it again.

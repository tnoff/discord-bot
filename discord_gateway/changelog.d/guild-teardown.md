`cleanup()` asks the commands pod to tear the guild down (`HttpCommandsClient.teardown_guild`, #1030) in place of clearing and blocking the downloader and search queues, discarding the dropped requests and deleting the guild's bundles itself. It is tried twice, one second apart; if the pod stays unreachable it logs a warning and carries on with the voice disconnect, the player's cleanup, the broker close and the player dir, leaving the guild's queued work to the result loop and its bundles to their 24h TTL. `BOT_SHUTDOWN` still skips it. Deploy the commands image first: until it serves the route, every teardown request fails and cleanup logs the warning.

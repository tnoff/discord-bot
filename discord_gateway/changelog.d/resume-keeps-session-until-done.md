- A gateway stopped part-way through resuming its saved player sessions no longer loses them. The session used to be deleted before the resume started, so a rolling restart or node loss in that window (the wait for the previous gateway, then the voice join) left nothing to resume on the next start, and the guild's queue could resurface on a later `!play`. The session now stays until the resume has an outcome: it is dropped when there is nothing to resume or when the attempt fails, and a successful resume leaves the fresh session the voice join wrote. A resume cancelled by shutdown keeps it.

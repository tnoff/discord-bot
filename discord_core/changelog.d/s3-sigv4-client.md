Replaced boto3 with a small S3 client of our own: `utils/integrations/s3.py` now signs its requests with AWS Signature Version 4 and sends them with `requests`, and boto3 is no longer a dependency. The public functions (`upload_file`, `get_file`, `list_objects`, `delete_file`), the exceptions and the digest helpers are unchanged. It reads the same `AWS_*` environment variables (keys, `AWS_SESSION_TOKEN`, `AWS_REGION`/`AWS_DEFAULT_REGION`, `AWS_ENDPOINT_URL_S3`/`AWS_ENDPOINT_URL`), always uses path-style addressing, and retries connection errors, 429 and 5xx. Timeouts and retries can be tuned with `S3_CONNECT_TIMEOUT`, `S3_READ_TIMEOUT`, `AWS_MAX_ATTEMPTS` and `S3_RETRY_BACKOFF`. Credentials now come only from those variables: there is no instance-role or `~/.aws` lookup any more.
